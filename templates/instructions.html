How to fill the Lab Staff Quality Feedback Form

  1. {{ icon('id') }}Login

    Enter your Student ID or Faculty ID and the security code.

  2. {{ icon('users') }}Pick lab staff

    You will see every lab staff member assigned to you. Click Give Feedback on each card.

  3. {{ icon('star') }}Rate 1 to 5

    Rate every criterion using the guide below and add optional remarks.

  4. {{ icon('check-circle') }}Preview & submit

    Check your answers, tick the declaration and click Final Submit.

{{ icon('info') }}Feedback is accepted only once per lab staff member and cannot be edited after submission.
